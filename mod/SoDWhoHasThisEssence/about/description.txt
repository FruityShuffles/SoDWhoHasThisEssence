[h1]Who Has This Essence?[/h1]
For co-op: see at a glance which essences you share with your teammates, and who could combine the essence lying in front of you.

[list]
[*][b]Tab Scoreboard:[/b] essences that you and a teammate both have equipped get a green outline.
[*][b]Essence pickup prompt:[/b] a green line under the essence's name lists the teammates who already have it equipped, e.g. [i](Player 2)[/i]. It isn't shown when you can combine that essence yourself.
[/list]

[h2]Setting[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Show duplicates between other players[/b] (off by default): also outline essences that two or more teammates share when you don't have them. Useful if you want to call out duplicates that your teammates have.
[/list]
